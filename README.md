# Tally – Gold ledger and profit tracker (WoW Forever)

A simple gold ledger for **WoW: Forever**. Tally shows where every copper came from and where it went, sorted by category, for this session, today, this week, this month or all time.

## Features

**Every copper counted**
- Sorts each change in your gold by what you were doing at the time: vendor, mailbox, auction house, trainer, flight master, trade, loot or quest.
- **Income:** looted gold, vendor sales, AH sales, quest rewards, mail and trades.
- **Expenses:** vendor purchases, repairs, AH purchases, AH deposits, training, flights, postage and trades.
- Works with the default auction house and with Auctionator.

**At a glance**
- Time played, **net profit or loss**, gold per hour and gold on hand.
- Looted items, valued at what a vendor pays, plus your net including them.
- **Biggest AH buy** and **biggest AH sale**, with the item name.
- Clear bar charts: one bar for the overall split, then a bar per category with its amount and share.

**Any time range**
- This session, Today, 7 days, 30 days or All time.
- **All characters:** see your whole account together.
- Recent transactions, looted items, past sessions and a day-by-day view.

**Kept for good**
- Daily totals and your last 2,000 transactions are saved for each character.
- `/reload` keeps your session going. A new login starts a new session.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/tally` | Open or close the ledger |
| `/tally today` / `week` / `month` / `all` / `session` | Open the ledger on a time range |
| `/tally reset` | Start a new session |
| `/tally scale 1.1` | Make the window bigger or smaller (1 = normal) |

## Install

1. Put the `Tally` folder in `World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns`.
2. **Fully quit and restart the game.** Forever doesn't load new addon code on `/reload`.
3. If it shows as out of date, tick "Load out of date AddOns".
4. Type `/tally`.
